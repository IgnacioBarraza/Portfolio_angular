My Journey

With over 2 years of experience in software development, I specialize in building scalable and efficient solutions using the PEAN stack (PostgreSQL, Express, Angular, Node.js). My approach emphasizes well-structured architectures, such as hexagonal design, to ensure maintainability and flexibility. I strive for clean, modular code while balancing technical excellence with practical, real-world application.

I believe in the Japanese concept of "Kaizen" (改善) - continuous improvement. Each project is an opportunity to refine my craft and expand my knowledge.

My development philosophy is inspired by "Wabi-sabi" (侘寂) - finding beauty in imperfection and accepting the natural cycle of growth and decay. This translates to creating software that is both beautiful and practical, embracing simplicity and authenticity.
